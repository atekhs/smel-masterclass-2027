Discounts and concessions

ConcessionConditionDiscount
Early-bird registrationFull payment received by 30 November 202610% off the registration fee
Group nomination3–5 delegates from one institution, registered together7.5% off each registration fee
Group nomination6 or more delegates from one institution12.5% off each registration fee
Alumni referralNominated by a confirmed 2027 delegate's institutionComplimentary Banquet seat for one accompanying guest

Discounts apply to the programme registration fee (USD 2,550) and are not combined; the most favourable single discount applies. For example, early-bird registration saves USD 255 per delegate, and a group of six or more saves USD 318.75 per delegate.

What is not included

  • International and domestic airfare.
  • Visa fees. Delegates should confirm current entry requirements with Rwanda's Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration before travel; many nationalities are eligible for visa-on-arrival or visa-free entry.
  • Travel and medical insurance (strongly recommended) and any required vaccination certificates, for example yellow fever depending on country of departure.
  • Dinners other than the Welcome Cocktail Reception and the Banquet; room service, laundry, minibar and other personal expenses.
  • Optional excursions and cultural evenings.

Notes on pricing

  • All prices are in United States Dollars for this edition. Accommodation rates are estimated on a group rate and will be confirmed with the Kigali Marriott Hotel; final invoices show the confirmed rates.
  • Sponsoring institutions may pay in local currency at the prevailing exchange rate on the date of payment, where the Secretariat's banking arrangements allow.
  • Prices exclude applicable bank charges, which are borne by the payer.
