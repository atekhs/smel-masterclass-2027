General objective

To strengthen the strategic, managerial and leadership capacity of top-level executives in the administrative, protocol and policy contingent of public and private institutions, enabling them to design and deliver better strategy, policy and services in a complex and fast-changing environment.

Specific objectives

By the end of the Masterclass, delegates will be able to:

  1. Think and plan strategicallyApply strategic foresight, scenario planning and strategy-execution frameworks to set clear institutional direction for three to ten years.
  2. Design and deliver effective policyUse evidence, stakeholder analysis and delivery-unit methods to move policy from intention to measurable outcome.
  3. Decide and lead under pressureMake sound decisions under uncertainty and lead teams through crisis, including security, reputational and operational emergencies.
  4. Command diplomacy, protocol and executive presenceConduct high-level engagements, official ceremonies and negotiations with confidence, precision and cultural intelligence.
  5. Steward resources with integrityRead financial statements and budgets strategically, strengthen value for money and accountability, and mobilise alternative financing.
  6. Lead digital and AI-enabled transformationAssess digital maturity, govern data and artificial intelligence responsibly, and lead the people side of change.
  7. Build high-performing teams and institutionsApply performance management, delegation, coaching and culture-building tools suited to public and corporate settings.
  8. Network and collaborate across borders and sectorsBuild durable professional relationships with peers across Africa.
  9. Translate learning into actionProduce and commit to a personal 100-day Leadership Action Plan for implementation on return.
