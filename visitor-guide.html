TopicGuidance
Entry and visasConfirm current requirements with Rwanda's Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration before travel. Many nationalities are eligible for visa-on-arrival or visa-free entry.
HealthTravel and medical insurance is strongly recommended. A yellow fever certificate may be required depending on your country of departure.
ClimateMild in late February, mid-20s °C by day with occasional rain. Pack light layers and an umbrella.
Time zoneCentral Africa Time (CAT, UTC+2), one hour ahead of West Africa Time.
MoneyThe Rwandan franc (RWF). Major cards are widely accepted at the hotel and in central Kigali.
Plastic bagsSingle-use plastic bags are restricted. Leave them out of your luggage.
UmugandaOn the last Saturday of the month (27 February), road movement is restricted from 08:00 to 11:00.
Getting aroundProgramme transport is provided for all visits. Airport transfers are included in residential packages.
