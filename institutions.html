The list applies across African national and sub-national governments.

Centre of government

  • Presidency, State House, Office of the Vice-President, Office of the Prime Minister
  • Office of the Head of the Civil Service and Public Service Commission
  • Office of the Secretary to the Government (Federation, State or equivalent)
  • Cabinet Office and Cabinet Secretariat
  • Government Houses and Offices of State Governors and Deputy Governors
  • Presidential and gubernatorial delivery, performance-monitoring and policy units

Legislatures

  • National Assembly, Senate and House of Representatives administrations; State and Provincial Houses of Assembly
  • Offices of the Clerk, legislative committees, research, library and budget offices
  • Offices of presiding officers and principal officers

Foreign affairs and protocol

  • Ministries of Foreign Affairs and Regional Integration; diplomatic missions and consulates
  • State Protocol Departments; Protocol Units of ministries, legislatures and governors' offices
  • Ministries of Information, Communication and National Orientation

Economic and finance

  • Ministries of Finance, Budget and National or Economic Planning
  • Ministries of Trade, Industry and Investment; investment-promotion agencies
  • Revenue authorities, customs services and debt-management offices
  • Central banks, public procurement authorities, audit and accountant-general offices
  • AfCFTA national coordination offices

Security and justice

  • Ministries of Defence, Interior, Internal Security and Police Affairs
  • Office of the National Security Adviser and national security councils
  • Armed forces, police, intelligence, civil defence, correctional, immigration and customs services
  • Ministries of Justice; anti-corruption, financial-intelligence and human-rights bodies

Social, infrastructure and digital

  • Ministries of Health, Education, Works, Transport, Power, Energy and Water
  • Ministries and agencies of Communications, Digital Economy and Innovation; e-government agencies
  • Ministries of Local Government; local-government service commissions
  • Environment, climate and disaster-management agencies

Regional and continental bodies

  • Secretariats and commissions of the African Union, ECOWAS, EAC, SADC, COMESA, ECCAS and IGAD
  • African development-finance institutions and their country offices
