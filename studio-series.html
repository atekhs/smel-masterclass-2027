Guiding questions

Studio conversations follow the programme's motto. Typical prompts include:

  • Statecraft: What does your principal or board need from you that they rarely ask for?
  • Strategy: Which decision in the next 100 days will matter most in five years?
  • Stewardship: What will your institution be able to do after you leave that it cannot do today?

Use of recordings

  • Speakers approve recordings before any release.
  • Delegates receive their own recordings for use within their institutions.
  • With consent, selected conversations may form part of the SMEL-Masterclass archive and the alumni portal.
  • No recording identifies anything said in Chatham House sessions.
