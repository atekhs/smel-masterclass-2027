Arc I: DirectionMonday 22 February 2027 · Morning, 10:30–13:30Takeaway: The SMEL One-Page Strategy Canvas

Purpose

To move delegates from short-term administration to long-range strategic thinking, and to close the notorious gap between strategy on paper and strategy delivered.

Course outline

  1. The strategic leader's mindsetThe difference between management, leadership and strategic leadership; the leader as sense-maker and direction-setter.
  2. Reading the environmentPESTEL, stakeholder mapping and horizon scanning for public and corporate institutions; identifying weak signals and megatrends shaping Africa to 2035.
  3. Strategic foresight and scenario planningBuilding plausible futures; the use of scenarios in national planning; stress-testing strategy against disruption.
  4. Crafting vision, mission and strategic prioritiesWriting direction that inspires and guides choice; aligning institutional strategy with national development plans, Agenda 2063 and the Sustainable Development Goals.
  5. From plan to results: strategy executionThe Balanced Scorecard, Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), logical frameworks and results chains; cascading strategy into departmental and individual targets.
  6. Performance contractingLessons from Rwanda's Imihigo and other African performance-contract systems; designing targets that are ambitious yet credible.
  7. Leading the strategy conversationBuilding ownership among staff, boards, legislators and partners.

Applied exercise

Syndicates draft a one-page strategic direction statement and a three-year scorecard for a real institution represented in the syndicate.

Key takeaway tool

The SMEL One-Page Strategy Canvas. A single sheet that holds direction, priorities, measures and owners, so a strategy can be read, tested and cascaded in one sitting.

Proposed faculty profile

A keynote on strategic foresight by a former head of a continental development institution, and a strategy-execution masterclass by a founding business-school scholar. Named faculty are announced only once their participation is confirmed in writing. See Faculty by Capstone.
