The Syndicate Strategy Project

A real or realistic institutional challenge, worked across the week and presented to a faculty panel on 27 February.

On the first day, each syndicate of 8 to 10 delegates receives an institutional challenge. Syndicates work on it across the week with a dedicated mentor, then present a strategy, an implementation plan and a risk assessment to a faculty panel. The best syndicate is recognised at the Banquet with the Ubumwe Laurel for the Best Syndicate.

Example challenges

  • Reducing the cost of a state visit by 30% without lowering standards.
  • Designing a delivery unit for a newly elected governor.
  • Preparing a national response plan for a cyber-attack on government systems.

Milestones

WhenMilestone
Mon 22 Feb, 17:45Project launch and challenge briefing

Fri 26 Feb, 17:15Final syndicate rehearsals
Sat 27 Feb, 11:30–15:30Presentations to the Faculty Panel and adjudication
Sat 27 Feb, eveningBest Syndicate recognised at the Banquet

What the panel looks for

  • A clear diagnosis of the problem and its stakeholders.
  • A strategy that makes real trade-offs.
  • An implementation plan with owners, milestones and measures.
  • A candid risk assessment with mitigations.
  • A presentation fit for a cabinet, a board or a principal.

The project counts for 25% of each delegate's assessment. See Assessment and Certification.
