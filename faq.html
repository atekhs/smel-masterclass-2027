Who should attend?

Senior officials and executives with at least 10 years of experience, including 5 in a senior management, advisory or command role, who currently lead a department, unit, office, programme, command or principal's office. See Eligibility.

How much does it cost?

USD 5,690 for the Standard Residential Package, USD 8,995 for Executive Residential and USD 5,210 for Non-Residential. See Fees and Packages.

Are there discounts?

Yes: 10% on the registration fee for full payment by 30 November 2026; 7.5% for groups of 3–5 and 12.5% for groups of 6 or more. Only the most favourable single discount applies.

When must I pay?

Full payment is due by 29 January 2027. Public institutions may secure places with an official purchase order or letter of commitment.

Can someone else take my place?

Yes. Another qualified official of the same institution may substitute at no charge until 12 February 2027.

What if I have to cancel?

80% refund on or before 15 January 2027; 50% from 16 January to 5 February 2027; no refund after that or for non-attendance.

Do I need a visa?

Many nationalities are eligible for visa-on-arrival or visa-free entry. Confirm current requirements with Rwanda's Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration before travel.

Is airfare included?

No. International and domestic airfare, visa fees, insurance, most dinners and personal expenses are not included.

What language is the programme in?

English. Simultaneous French interpretation can be arranged for Francophone delegations on request, subject to minimum numbers.

What do I receive at the end?

The Certificate of Completion in Strategic Management and Executive Leadership, for delegates with at least 85% attendance who complete all assessed components, with Distinction for outstanding performance.

Can I bring a spouse or guest?

Yes. A Banquet seat for a guest is USD 620 and the city tour is USD 520. Additional hotel nights are available.

Are sessions recorded?

No. Sessions follow the Chatham House Rule. Recording happens only in the Interview Studio, by booking and with the speaker's approval.
