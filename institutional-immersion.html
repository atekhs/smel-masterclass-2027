Institutional Immersion I: Governance and Performance Delivery

Guided study visits to leading Rwandan public institutions on governance, performance contracting and service delivery.

Learning focus

  • How performance contracts (Imihigo) are set, monitored and reviewed.
  • How citizen feedback reaches decision-makers and changes services.
  • How delivery is organised, measured and reported across levels of government.

Format

Delegates travel in syndicate groups. Each visit opens with a briefing by host officials, continues with questions and, where possible, a walk through the service in operation. The afternoon debrief uses peer-consulting sets: delegates bring a problem from their own institution and test what they have seen against it.

Links to the curriculum

The immersion applies Capstone 1 (performance contracting and strategy execution) and Capstone 2 (delivery units, home-grown governance innovation).

Host institutions are subject to confirmation. The Secretariat will publish confirmed sites in the final agenda by 31 January 2027.
