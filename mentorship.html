During the Masterclass

Each syndicate is assigned a Syndicate Mentor, a senior practitioner who coaches the group through the Strategy Project and meets each member one-to-one to review their 100-day Leadership Action Plan.

WhenMentor contact
Mon 22 FebSyndicate Strategy Project launch
Tue 23 FebWorking session 1
Thu 25 FebWorking session 2
Fri 26 FebOne-to-one reviews of action plans; final rehearsals
Sat 27 FebPresentations to the Faculty Panel

After the Masterclass

Delegates may opt into a 90-day virtual mentoring circle with monthly online check-ins, alongside the 30-, 60- and 100-day reviews of their action plans.

Become a mentor

Senior practitioners with experience in government, security, protocol, finance or enterprise leadership who would like to mentor a syndicate may write to the Secretariat at info@afroindustrygroup.africa.
