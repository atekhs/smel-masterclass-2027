The return to your institution

  • Stronger strategy documents and sharper policy advice.
  • More disciplined protocol and better-managed crises.
  • Officials motivated by the recognition that they are invested in.
  • A 100-day Leadership Action Plan for each official, reviewed at 30, 60 and 100 days.
  • Institutional artefacts (strategy canvases, memoranda, scorecards) that stay with the institution.

Group nominations

Nominating three or more officials lets learning be applied collectively on return.

Group sizeConcession on registration fee
3 – 5 delegates7.5% each
6 or more delegates12.5% each

Public-sector commitments

A place is secured on full payment, or on an official purchase order or letter of commitment from a public institution. Institutions may pay in local currency where banking arrangements allow. Substitution of a nominated official by another qualified official is free until 12 February 2027.
