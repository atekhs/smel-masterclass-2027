Ubumwe is the Kinyarwanda word for unity, a value at the heart of Rwanda's national renewal. The laurel is the ancient emblem of honour bestowed on those who serve with distinction. Together, the name expresses the conviction that excellent leadership unites institutions, sectors, nations and generations.

Recipients are styled Laureates of Ubumwe, for example “Ubumwe Laureate for Strategic Leadership, 2027”.

The three orders

Order I

Laurels of Honour

Conferred on distinguished leaders and institutions beyond the cohort, on the recommendation of the Laurels Council.

The insignia

The insignia is a gold laurel wreath encircling a stylised silhouette of rolling hills, with the inscription UBUMWE · STATECRAFT · STRATEGY · STEWARDSHIP and the year of conferment. Its palette of deep blue, gold and green draws on the sky, sun and hills of the host nation while remaining distinct from any national emblem. The Laurels Council will commission the final design from an African designer and approve it; the mark shown here is illustrative.

What each laureate receives

  • An engraved Ubumwe Laurel trophy
  • A citation scroll
  • A lapel insignia
  • Order I laureates also receive a framed commendation

Selection is overseen by an independent Laurels Council. See Selection and Integrity.
