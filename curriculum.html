The academic core of the Masterclass is six capstones arranged in three learning arcs, one per capstone day. Each arc is followed by an application day on which delegates test what they learned through institutional immersion, syndicate work and simulation.

Anatomy of a capstone

Each capstone runs for about three and a half contact hours, in four movements:

  1. Keynote framingA lead facilitator sets the strategic context.
  2. Masterclass lecture with an African caseCore frameworks and evidence, anchored in a real institution.
  3. Applied exerciseDelegates use the tools on their own institutions' problems.
  4. Leaders' dialogueA senior practitioner responds, in a moderated conversation, to delegates' real challenges.
