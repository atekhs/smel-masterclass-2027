Evening Fireside: Lessons from a Life in Public Service

Monday 22 February · 19:30–21:00 · Executive lounge

A fireside conversation with a distinguished African public servant on decisions, setbacks and the habits that sustained a long career. The format is informal and the conversation is held under the Chatham House Rule. Dinner is at leisure.

Executive Wellness and Resilience for Leaders

Wednesday 24 February · 18:30–19:30 · Wellness area · Optional

A practical workshop that follows the week's most intense day of crisis simulation and protocol clinic. It covers managing stress and fatigue, sleep and travel, sustaining judgement under pressure, and protecting health across a demanding term of office.
