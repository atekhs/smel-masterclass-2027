How booking works

  1. RequestSend the form below to the Secretariat with your preferred format and day.
  2. ConfirmationThe Secretariat confirms a slot that does not clash with your sessions.
  3. RecordingArrive ten minutes early. A producer briefs you on the questions.
  4. ReviewYou review the recording and approve, request changes or withdraw it.

Media accreditation

Accredited journalists may attend the Opening Ceremony and the Banquet, and interview speakers in the studio by arrangement. Plenary and syndicate sessions are closed to media. Apply by 5 February 2027.

This form does not send anything by itself. It assembles your details into a message you can copy and email to the Secretariat at info@afroindustrygroup.africa.
