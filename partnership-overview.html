Institutional partnerships

Strategic engagement has begun with host-country governance, management, development and convention bodies, with the University of Rwanda, with leading African business schools, and with professional bodies in management, public administration and protocol. All institutional partnerships are subject to agreement and will be announced as they are concluded.

Our partnership principles

  • Honours are never for sale. No Laurel may be purchased or conditioned on sponsorship; sponsorship confers acknowledgement only.
  • The room stays candid. Partners respect the Chatham House Rule in all closed sessions.
  • Association is dignified. Recognition follows the protocol standards of a state-style event.
