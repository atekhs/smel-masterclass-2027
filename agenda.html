Before 20 February: pre-arrival phase

  • Confirmation of registration and payment
  • Visa and travel guidance
  • Online onboarding through the delegate portal
  • Diagnostic self-assessment and pre-reading pack
  • Submission of each delegate's institutional challenge
  • Syndicate allocation by the Secretariat

Agenda notes

  • Umuganda. Rwanda's monthly community-work morning falls on Saturday 27 February (the last Saturday of the month). It will be confirmed with the Rwandan authorities closer to the date; the Saturday schedule is built around it.
  • Study visits are subject to confirmation by host institutions. Sites may change without affecting the learning objectives.
  • The agenda is indicative and may be adjusted by the Secretariat to accommodate faculty and dignitary schedules. A final agenda will be issued to delegates by 31 January 2027.
