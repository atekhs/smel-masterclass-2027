The convening group

AI-Group Inc. Holdings Ltd, Rwanda, convenes and organises the Masterclass, and promotes it with Africa Industrialization Group Inc., Canada. The Group brings together capability in executive education, leadership development, strategic advisory and event management.

The Laurels Council

An independent council of five to seven eminent persons oversees selection of the Ubumwe Laurels. Its membership will be announced once confirmed. See Selection and Integrity.
