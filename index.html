Leading beyond position

Offices are temporary. Institutions must persist.

The SMEL-Masterclass condenses the essential disciplines of a senior executive MBA (strategy, policy, decision-making, finance, diplomacy and digital transformation) into six intensive residential days. It is built for the Permanent Secretaries, Directors, Chiefs of Staff, Heads of Protocol, security leaders and corporate executives who turn a principal's direction into results.
