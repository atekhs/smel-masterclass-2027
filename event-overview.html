The Strategic Management & Executive Leadership Masterclass (SMEL-Masterclass) is the inaugural edition of a continental programme for the officials and executives who carry Africa's most consequential institutional responsibilities. It condenses the essential disciplines of a senior executive MBA into six intensive days, built for leaders who work where administration, protocol and policy meet.

Statecraft, Strategy and Stewardship

The edition's motto, Statecraft, Strategy and Stewardship, is carried by its sub-theme: Leading Institutions with Foresight, Integrity and Impact in Africa's Decade of Transformation. The full programme is convened under the theme “Leading Beyond Position: Strategic Intelligence, Executive Authority, Institutional Performance & Transformational Leadership for Africa's Next Generation of Leaders.”

The idea behind the theme is simple. Offices are temporary; institutions must persist. A leader is judged by the strength, integrity and performance of the institution they leave behind, as much as by the authority they held while in office.

What the six days contain

  • Six capstones delivered in three learning arcs: Direction, Action and Legacy.
  • Two institutional immersions in Kigali, covering governance and performance delivery, and the city's innovation and investment ecosystem.
  • A syndicate strategy project that runs across the week and ends before a faculty panel.
  • A reflective learning visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial and a guided tour of the capital.
  • A graduation and award ceremony, with the investiture of the Ubumwe Laurels of Executive Distinction.
  • A state-style gala banquet, the Thousand Hills Leadership Banquet.
