Distinguished Leaders of Government and Enterprise, Honourable Appointees, Officers of our Security Institutions, Esteemed Partners and Friends,

On behalf of the Board, Management, and AI-Group Inc. Holdings Ltd., I am pleased to introduce the inaugural SMEL-Masterclass, a Strategic Management & Executive Leadership Masterclass. It will be held in Kigali, Rwanda, from 22 to 27 February 2027.

Africa's history is defined by leadership: the men and women who have carried the aspirations of nations through the arduous transition from colonial rule to sovereignty, from institution-building to economic development, and from national independence to the ongoing pursuit of continental integration and prosperity. The generations that built modern Africa recognised that political independence could attain substantive significance only when supported by capable institutions, disciplined administration, visionary leadership, and a citizenry able to transform national resources into shared prosperity. The nature of leadership required in the twenty-first century, however, is changing profoundly.

The African executive now functions at the confluence of governance, economics, technology, investment, diplomacy, security, infrastructure, industrialisation, and global competitiveness. The office has become more complex, the decisions more consequential, and the margin for institutional failure significantly narrower. In this context, the SMEL-Masterclass has been conceived and is being promoted by AI-Group Inc. Holdings Ltd., Rwanda, and Africa Industrialization Group Inc., Canada. It reflects our conviction that Africa's next developmental chapter must be accompanied by deliberate investment in the intellectual, strategic, and ethical capacity of those entrusted with leading its institutions.

Why now: a continent at the threshold of a new executive era

We stand at a significant juncture in Africa's developmental trajectory. The continent is endowed with a youthful and increasingly sophisticated populace, abundant natural and human resources, expanding markets, entrepreneurial vitality, and a growing ambition to play a more decisive role in global economic affairs. At the same time, African leaders are addressing challenges of unprecedented complexity: technological disruption such as artificial intelligence, infrastructure deficits, industrialisation, investment mobilisation, regional integration, economic resilience, public sector reform, geopolitical rivalry, climate pressure, security, and the need for more effective institutions.

These realities call for a new standard of executive preparedness. The Permanent Secretary must transcend administrative duties; the Director must go beyond supervision; the political appointee must embody more than representation; the Head of Protocol must understand more than ceremony; the security professional must see beyond immediate threats; and the corporate executive must understand more than the confines of a single enterprise. Each must grasp the whole picture, understand the implications of decisions, coordinate diverse interests, and translate strategy into measurable outcomes.

This epoch demands leaders who can integrate wisdom with evidence, authority with accountability, ambition with discipline, innovation with prudence, and vision with execution. The SMEL-Masterclass is conceived as a practical response to this continental leadership imperative.

Leading beyond position: building institutions that outlive individuals

The Masterclass is convened under the theme “Leading Beyond Position: Strategic Intelligence, Executive Authority, Institutional Performance & Transformational Leadership for Africa's Next Generation of Leaders.” It embodies a core principle of sustainable nation-building: while offices are temporary, institutions must persist. Leadership should be judged not solely by the authority held but by the strength, integrity, and performance of the institution left behind.

SMEL serves the senior leaders in the public and private sectors who bear this responsibility, and aims to strengthen both individual leadership and institutional resilience through strategic management, governance, policy intelligence, financial literacy, negotiation, communication, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, risk management, and strategic execution. Its goal is better decisions, stronger teams, coherent policies, disciplined execution, resilient organisations, and leaders who can guide Africa's economic and institutional future.

A call to the architects of Africa's next chapter

The greatest legacy of leadership is not the leader's prominence but the enduring capacity created within the institution and society after the leader has moved on.

The SMEL-Masterclass is therefore offered not merely as a programme of executive instruction; it is an invitation to reflection, renewal and responsibility. We invite African governments, Ministries, Departments and Agencies, corporations, development institutions, professional bodies and strategic enterprises to invest deliberately in the people who carry their most consequential responsibilities. We invite our executives to enter the Masterclass not merely as accomplished professionals, but as students of a continent whose future demands continual learning, intellectual courage and disciplined execution.

Let us build leaders who can see beyond the immediate, think beyond the conventional, serve beyond themselves and build beyond their tenure. Let us cultivate leaders who see leadership not as the privilege of a higher position, but as a serious duty to bear greater responsibility for the people, institutions, and future generations they serve. Kigali 2027 should go beyond being a Masterclass venue; it should become a hub for exchanging ideas, sharing experiences, embracing responsibility, and reaffirming African executive purpose.

When we enhance the quality of our leaders, we fortify the institutions under their stewardship. Strengthening those institutions broadens the prospects available to our economies. And when we expand those opportunities through wisdom, integrity, and courage, we advance the sustained development of an Africa that is confident in its abilities, deliberate in its decisions, and equipped to shape its own future.

See you in Kigali.

Carl Oshodi-Isibor, Ph.D., DBA
Group CEO, AI-Group Inc. Holdings Ltd
