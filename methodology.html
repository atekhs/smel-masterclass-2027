Learning philosophy

Senior executives learn most when the content meets their experience. The design follows adult-learning principles: delegates bring extensive practical knowledge, want immediate relevance, and learn best through challenge, peer exchange and reflection. Faculty act as provocateurs, coaches and moderators more than as lecturers, and each method is chosen to produce a specific shift in how delegates think or behave.
